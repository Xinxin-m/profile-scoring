The finding that matters most for a web-coding screen

98 of 160 skills give you no evidence of web ability at all, and the two judges agree on that call 136/160 of the time (85%). If applicants pick three favourites freely, a strong candidate can hand you three excellent skills that tell you nothing about the axis you care about most. Require at least one web-facing submission, and keep reporting the flag separately so a high total cannot disguise an absent signal.
